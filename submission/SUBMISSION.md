**Description:**

The best — direct booking app, revenue management and self check-in, $9.4M — ranks first in every scenario: NPV +$3.3M (Low −$5.2M, High +$12.3M), capital back in 2031. Every bigger plan is worth less. All numbers come from the provided dataset.
Each page shows one message; a Hotel Explorer, an investment explorer (comparison, timeline, scenarios) and three "why" panels open on demand.
